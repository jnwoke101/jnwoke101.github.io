06 / Lessons from the lab

Passing isn't the whole story.

⚑

Point-in-time compliance versus persistent enforcement

The final Tenable scan showed all ten selected findings as Passed. However, three advanced audit subcategories— WN11-AU-000150, WN11-AU-000555, and WN11-AU-000560— were observed reverting to No Auditing after Group Policy refresh or VM restart. The automated auditpol remediations demonstrated an active compliant configuration during the final scan; persistent policy enforcement remains an identified follow-up. This case study does not present the passing scan as proof of restart persistence.
