ABOUT / 001
Who I am

IT experience applied to cyber defense.

I earned a B.S. in Cybersecurity from the University of Maryland Global Campus. My professional background in end-user support informs how I approach incidents and vulnerabilities: understand the environment, verify the impact, implement the change, and document the result.

Through my cybersecurity internship and independent labs, I have worked on vulnerability assessments, Windows security configurations, SIEM detection, endpoint investigations, and Azure infrastructure.

My approach

Evidence before conclusions.

I prefer projects that show the full lifecycle—not just the final screenshot. My writeups connect an initial observation to the technical steps, verification results, and limitations discovered along the way.

  • Investigate alerts and document the supporting telemetry.
  • Prioritize findings and validate remediation with rescans.
  • Build repeatable PowerShell and cloud lab workflows.
  • Communicate technical outcomes clearly to other teams.
